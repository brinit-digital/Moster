Trolley Karaoke Speaker Double 8" Speaker System

The MOSTER Trolley Karaoke Speaker Double 8" System delivers powerful 120W RMS audio with heavy bass, perfect for portable entertainment. Featuring a built-in rechargeable battery, wireless UHF mic, TWS pairing, and 7-LED light patterns, it’s ideal for dynamic karaoke sessions anywhere.

Portable Karaoke Powerhouse

Experience robust sound with the MOSTER Double 8" Trolley Speaker, featuring 120W RMS output and heavy bass. Its built-in rechargeable battery and trolley design make it ideal for outdoor events or home karaoke sessions.

Versatile Connectivity and Features

Connect via USB, mSD, AUX, or FM, and enjoy hands-free singing with the wireless UHF mic and mobile holder. The TWS function allows pairing with another speaker for enhanced audio.
